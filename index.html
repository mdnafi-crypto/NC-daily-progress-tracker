👋 How to understand this page

These charts turn your task records into pictures. Higher usually means better progress. Empty days mean you did not record any tasks—not that you failed.

1. Choose a time period.2. Look at the chart.3. Tap More info if something is confusing.

📈 Your daily progress

How much of your planned tasks you completed each day.

More info

Each point represents one day. A point near the top means you completed most of that day's tasks. A point near the bottom means fewer tasks were completed. Gaps mean there were no tasks recorded for that day. Watch the overall direction, not just one bad day.

Data & calculation notes

Task completion rate = completed ÷ planned tasks. Time achievement = actual minutes ÷ planned minutes; it is shown separately and is not treated as a productivity score. A day with tasks but zero completions is 0%. A day with no tasks is shown as “No data” and excluded from averages. Pending tasks remain planned until marked completed or missed. Recurring tasks are generated for dates as you visit them; historical records are retained.
