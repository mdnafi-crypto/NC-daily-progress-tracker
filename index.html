Data & calculation notes

Task completion rate = completed ÷ planned tasks. Time achievement = actual minutes ÷ planned minutes; it is shown separately and is not treated as a productivity score. A day with tasks but zero completions is 0%. A day with no tasks is shown as “No data” and excluded from averages. Pending tasks remain planned until marked completed or missed. Recurring tasks are generated for dates as you visit them; historical records are retained.
